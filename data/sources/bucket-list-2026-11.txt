 With a toddler in mind
 Verified features worth knowing: Alpenglow has Bend's only free splash pad (summer). Larkspur, Juniper, Pine Nursery, Alpenglow
 and Farewell Bend have all-abilities playgrounds. In Redmond, Sam Johnson's Hope Playground has a dedicated baby and
 toddler area.

Worth the drive (1)
     90 Smith Rock State Park 9241 NE Crooked River Dr,
         Terrebonne
         No playground, but the most spectacular park near Bend:
         towering rock spires and river trails. About 35-40 min.
         Keep little ones close, there are steep drops.
