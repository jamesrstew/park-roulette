# Park audit A (37 Bend parks), November 18, 2026

## Summary

- Parks checked: 37 (all Bend Park & Recreation District; each checked against its BPRD park page, OSM via Overpass for playgrounds, toilets, and the park polygon within 150 to 500 m, plus BPRD news and project pages).
- Verified OK, no changes: 15
- Need correction: 19 parks, 27 proposals. Five are critical (status, play type, splash pad, restrooms, or location); the rest are missing features.
- Couldn't fully verify: 3 (see below)

### Critical corrections

1. **columbia-park-bend: status active to closed.** BPRD closed the park, playground included, on Oct. 19, 2026, through early summer 2027 for the river access project and Gilchrist Bridge replacement.
2. **discovery-park-bend: play none to nature, and remove splash_pad.** It has a natural play area with hand-pump water courses, not a splash pad. BPRD lists no Playground. Alpenglow is the only BPRD sprayground.
3. **first-street-rapids-park-bend: play none to nature.** It is on BPRD's natural play areas list.
4. **hillside-park-ii-bend: remove restrooms.** BPRD lists none (trash can and dog station only). Medium confidence; check in person.
5. **little-fawn-park-bend: move the pin.** It sits about 165 m from the geocoded address. Medium confidence; verify on aerial imagery.

## Couldn't fully verify

- **goodrich-pasture-park-bend:** BPRD lists the feature as "Playground" but describes "exploratory play areas" with stones, and OSM has no playground mapped. It is unclear whether there is a conventional structure or only natural/exploratory play. Keep play=playground (official listing) and check in person.
- **bend-whitewater-park-bend:** The pin and address (166 SW Shevlin Hixon Dr) are McKay Park, next to the whitewater channel. The restrooms and picnic features belong to McKay Park (OSM toilets about 93 m away); the BPRD whitewater page does not list amenities. Play=none is correct. Consider noting that the amenities are at adjacent McKay Park.
- **jaycee-park-bend:** "picnic" is not mentioned on the BPRD page (lawn, half court, playground, seasonal restroom). Low impact; check in person.
