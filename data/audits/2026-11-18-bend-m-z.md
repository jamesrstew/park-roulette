# Park Roulette audit, batch B (37 parks, all Bend / BPRD)

Checked November 18, 2026, against each park's bendparksandrec.org page (description and "Features" amenity list), the BPRD Playground, Natural Play Area, and Water Play Feature listings, BPRD project pages, and OpenStreetMap (Overpass: playgrounds, toilets, dog parks, picnic tables, and containment checks of each coordinate against the park polygon).

**Headline findings**

- **Skyline Sports Complex:** our data says no playground. BPRD's own page says "small playground" and lists Playground. OSM has none, and a crowd report says none. Proposed: playground, medium confidence, **check in person**.
- **Sylvan Park:** the playground is under renovation and not finished. BPRD expects it to reopen in late spring 2027 (surfacing delayed by cold). It should not be listed as an open playground.
- **Stone Creek Park:** the in-park play is natural play only. The swings and structures in OSM nearby belong to Silver Rail Elementary. Proposed: nature, medium confidence, check in person.
- **Sawyer Park:** the parking lot has been closed for construction since October 26, 2026 (through about summer 2027).
- **Tillicum / Chase Ranch** has no amenities, so `picnic` is wrong.
- **No splash pads** in this batch. The "Water Play Feature" at Rockridge and Stone Creek is a hand-pump water course or trough, not a splash pad. Nothing should be tagged `splash_pad`.
- **Play type for every other park** matches BPRD. All 21 parks we list as `playground` (other than Stone Creek) are on BPRD's playground list. None of the 15 `none` parks are, except Skyline.
- **Town, name, status:** all correct. Every park is BPRD-operated and public. Pacific Crest Athletic Fields sits on school land and is open only outside school hours.
- **Coordinates:** all within 150 m of the park. Riley Ranch is the loosest (136 m outside the OSM polygon, at the Glen Vista trailhead), still within tolerance.
