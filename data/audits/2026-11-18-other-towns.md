# Park Roulette audit: Redmond, Prineville, Sisters, Sunriver, La Pine, Madras, Culver, Terrebonne, Crooked River Ranch

Audited November 18, 2026. Sources: official owner pages (City of Redmond facility directory, CCPRD, SPRD, SROA, LPPRD, City of Madras, Oregon State Parks), OpenStreetMap (Overpass, one batched query for leisure=playground/park/dog_park over the whole area), then local news (Nugget, Redmond Spokesman, Madras Pioneer, KTVZ, Bend Magazine). Machine-readable proposals: /tmp/pr-park-audit-o.json.

## Summary

- **Parks checked:** 34
- **Verified OK, no change needed:** 6 (Crooked River, Gervais, Yellowpine, Pioneer, Stryker, Frontier Heritage). Pioneer shade and Stryker courts/picnic are unconfirmed, not wrong.
- **Need correction:** 28. Almost all are missing features the official pages list, mostly restrooms, picnic, parking and trail. The substantive fixes are:
  - **Quartz Park:** the note says "next to the skate park." That is wrong; Quartz has skate dots, and the skatepark is about 2 km away.
  - **Fort Rock:** the "treehouse" and "spinning net climber" in the note have no source.
  - **Village Green:** the note reads as if the play structure is still pending. It was replaced in spring 2020.
  - **Smith Rock:** `sports_courts` is wrong, and a construction advisory runs December 1, 2026 to May 31, 2027.
  - **Names:** Juniper Hills (city spelling) and Finley Butte Sports Complex & Park.
  - **Centennial:** add restrooms (open year-round).
  - **Sam Johnson:** add zipline (dual zip lines).
- **No high-confidence errors in play type, town, coordinates or status.** Every coordinate lands on the park (all within about 100 m of OSM features). Every `playground` claim is backed by an official page or OSM, except Culver.
- **Couldn't verify:** 5 key facts. Culver's playground, the Coffield playground's current status, Panorama Park's ownership and access, Fort Rock's specific equipment, and an official source for the High Desert Sports Complex playground.
- **Missing parks:** 3 public parks (Sisters Community Park, Crescent Park and Bean Park in Madras) and 2 possible ones where public access is unclear (Ochoco Pointe in Prineville, McKenzie Meadow Village in Sisters).

## Couldn't verify (check in person)

- **culver-city-park-culver: playground.** Only a Yelp review mentions playgrounds. Neither the Madras Pioneer nor Dreamland mentions play equipment, and OSM maps none in the park. The splash pad, restrooms and picnic areas are confirmed (Madras Pioneer, October 30, 2025). The skatepark, pump track and pickleball opened October 26, 2025. Do not feature the playground until someone has seen it.
- **coffield-community-center-playground-sisters: status.**
  - The playground is in OSM and in the Nugget's 2019 description of the grounds.
  - SPRD moved its hub to 611 E Cascade in 2025 and re-signed a Coffield lease with the school district in June 2025. SPRD's parks page no longer mentions a playground there.
  - It may still be open, but this is unconfirmed.
- **panorama-park-crooked-river-ranch: access.** The playground (OSM) and restrooms (secondary sources) are supported. No official page says whether the park is owned by the Crooked River Ranch Club & Maintenance Association or open to non-members. A realtor calls it a "county park." The note's caution stands.
- **fort-rock-park-sunriver: equipment.** The playground and restrooms are confirmed. SROA says Fort Rock and Paulina are open to owners and visitors; only Mary McCallum Park is owners-only. Swings, the spinner and the treehouse theme have no source.
- **high-desert-sports-complex-redmond: playground.** It is in OSM (a recent edit) and in a third-party listing, but the RAPRD pages list no amenities.
- **centennial-park-redmond.** OSM tags a playground polygon inside the park. The city's amenity list has no play structure, so the polygon is probably the splash plaza. Keep play=none.
- **sahalee-park-madras: splash pad.** Only an aggregator lists one, and a 2019 KBND story says it was being fundraised. The city's amenity list does not include it, so do not add it yet.
- **frontier-heritage-park-la-pine: splash pad.** It appears in the 2026-27 LPPRD budget as a planned install. Do not add it until it is built.
